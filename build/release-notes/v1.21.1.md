DigiByte miners no longer show up as Bitcoin

- **Fixed: miners on a DigiByte pool could be filed under the wrong coin.** If your miners point at a DGB pool that Baller didn't recognise by name — hmpool's `digi.` addresses were the case that surfaced this — they'd be grouped as Bitcoin, with Bitcoin block odds to match. Baller now also reads your DigiByte payout address directly, so it identifies DGB correctly on *any* pool, including ones that don't exist yet. Nothing to change on your end: the coin re-detects on the next poll after you update.
- **Note:** if you ever pinned a device's coin by hand (the `chain` dropdown on the device page), that pin still wins over auto-detection by design. Set it back to `auto` if you've since moved that miner to a different coin.
